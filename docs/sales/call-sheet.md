# Call sheet: keep this in front of you while dialing

Full version: `outreach-script.md`. **Goal:** book a 20-min call. Don't sell.
**Offer:** free for 90 days, then a locked-in founder price, for 3 Twin Cities shops.

---

### 1. Opener (pick the angle)

> Hi, is this **[owner]**? Nathan, local, in **[city]**. I'll be quick.

**A: After-hours.** For 24/7, emergency and drain shops, and HVAC in winter.
> I'm building an AI receptionist just for HVAC and plumbing shops. After hours
> it answers, sorts real emergencies from "can wait till morning," and texts you
> or your on-call tech the details instead of leaving it on voicemail.

**B: Missed calls.** For busy shops and owners on jobs all day.
> When you're on a job and the phone rings, what happens to that call?
> *(let them answer)* I'm building an AI receptionist that picks up every call,
> books it or takes the details, and texts you a summary, so they don't call the
> next shop on Google.

### 2. The ask

> I'm not selling anything yet. I'm looking for three local shops to help shape
> it, and they'd get it free for 90 days. Could I get 20 minutes to hear how calls
> work at your shop today?

### 3. Objections

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| "We have an answering service" | "Perfect, that's who I want to talk to. What do you like about it, and what drives you nuts?" |
| "Calls just go to my cell" | "How often does that wake you up for something that could've waited?" |
| "My wife / office handles it" | "Great. Could I talk to both of you? I want to know where it gets hard for them." |
| "We don't miss many calls" | "Do you know how many hit voicemail in a week? Most shops I talk to don't track it." |
| "Is this AI? Customers hate robots" | "Fair. That's exactly what I need your help getting right. What would make it OK?" |
| "Send me something" | "Happy to. What's the best email? Can I pencil in 20 min Thursday so it doesn't get lost?" |
| "Not interested" | "No problem. Mind if I check back in spring?" Then log it as `pass`. |

### 4. Close

> What works better, **[day] morning** or **[day] afternoon**? I'll text you to confirm.

### 5. Voicemail (under 20 sec, then send the email the same day)

> Hi [owner], Nathan, local to the Twin Cities. I'm building an AI receptionist
> for HVAC and plumbing shops and looking for three local shops to try it free.
> 20 minutes of your take would help a lot. **[number]**. Again, **[number]**.

---

**After every dial:** update the status in `prospects.md` and note which angle you used.
**Best times:** Tue–Thu, mid-morning. Avoid Monday mornings and the first cold snap or heat wave.
