# Design-partner prospects — Twin Cities metro

First pass, built 2026-10-25 from web directories (Angi, HomeAdvisor, Expertise,
BBB-style listings) and company self-descriptions. **Nothing here is verified
yet.** Before calling, check each shop's Google Business profile (phone, hours,
review count) and its license on the MN DLI lookup:
https://secure.doli.state.mn.us/lookup/licensing.aspx

**Ideal design partner:** independent (not a franchise), roughly 2–15 trucks, the
owner or a spouse still answers the phone, and the shop does emergency or
after-hours work. Franchises (Mr. Rooter, Roto-Rooter, Benjamin Franklin, Aire
Serv) are left out because their phone setup is usually decided by corporate.

Status values: `new` → `contacted` → `call booked` → `discovery done` → `pilot` / `pass`
